# Getting help

- **Documentation**: https://OWNER.github.io/runway/docs
- **Questions, ideas, show and tell**: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/OWNER/runway/discussions)
- **Bugs and feature requests**: [issues](https://github.com/OWNER/runway/issues/new/choose)
- **Security**: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

Before asking, `runway doctor --stage <stage>` checks credentials, APIs,
permissions and prerequisites, and most errors print a hint with the fix.
